# Listening scenes

These original images were generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-09-26 for filo's menu bar panel.
They follow the selected home-studio mockup's daylight, pale left text area, and warm equipment on the right.
The images contain no UI; all text, controls, materials, and accessibility elements are native.

| File | Rate family | Subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cd.png` | Through 44.1 kHz | Portable CD player and disc |
| `studio.png` | Above 44.1 through 48 kHz | Home recording studio |
| `hifi.png` | Above 48 through 96 kHz | Tube amplifier and bookshelf speaker |
| `reference.png` | Above 96 kHz | Horn speakers and monoblock amplifiers |

These are decorative listening scenes, not claims about audible quality or required equipment.
Each PNG is 1335 × 1178 pixels and shares the panel's aspect ratio.
The app downsamples to at most 680 pixels and keeps at most two decoded scenes in a cache with a 4 MiB cost limit.
No image is requested while the panel is closed, and no images are downloaded at runtime.
